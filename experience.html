Paris, France

Research Intern · Mathematical Modelling and Data Analysis

Institut du Cerveau — Paris Brain Institute

Developing Gaussian hidden Markov models and quantitative analysis pipelines in Python for experimental time series data.

Work on the data

  • Fit Gaussian hidden Markov models to multimodal experimental time series by maximum likelihood estimation, treating the observable signal as generated by an unobserved discrete state process.
  • Recover the most likely hidden state sequence using Viterbi decoding, producing per-timepoint latent state assignments from the fitted model.
  • Extract and analyse transition probabilities between latent states and dwell times within them, which is the quantity of scientific interest rather than the raw signal.
  • Build reproducible preprocessing pipelines: cleaning, windowing and validating experimental recordings before model fitting.
  • Communicate results to researchers, translating scientific questions into tractable statistical and computational formulations.

Hidden Markov models are a stochastic modelling technique shared with operations research, where they underpin condition monitoring and degradation modelling. The estimation problem is an optimisation problem.
