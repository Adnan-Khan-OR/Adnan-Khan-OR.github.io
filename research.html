07

Component-Level Reliability Analysis from Observational Repair Data

Estimated 500-hour reliability across five product models using Kaplan–Meier survival analysis with bootstrap uncertainty. Corrected censoring and selection bias through inverse probability of censoring weighting and standardisation, enabling causal comparison between product models and repair centres.

Survival AnalysisKaplan–MeierIPCWCausal Inference
December 2025

Energy Efficiency Prediction of Buildings

Group project for the data analysis and machine learning course. Benchmarked five regression models on 768 samples with eight architectural features to predict heating and cooling loads. A two-stage approach predicting cooling load from heating load reduced cooling-load error by more than half under cross-validation.
