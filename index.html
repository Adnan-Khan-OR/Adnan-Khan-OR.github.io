Hello, I am Adnan.

I build mathematical models that help organisations decide things. Which vehicle takes which route. How much capacity to install before anyone knows what demand will be. When to replace a machine that is quietly getting worse.

I am finishing a master’s in Operations Research and Risk Analytics at CentraleSupélec, and I arrived at it from the factory floor. Before Paris I spent my industrial engineering degree in Dhaka timing production lines and tracing defects back to their causes. That is where I learned that most operational problems are not really about the machines. They are about the decisions made around them.

What I like about optimisation is that it turns an argument into a number. Two people can disagree about which schedule is better, or they can write down the cost and find out.

This site is where I have written that work up properly: the problem, the model, the scale, and what it actually decided. Have a look around.

On this site

This site documents the optimisation work behind my curriculum vitae. Each project is written the way I would explain it to a colleague: what the problem was, why it was worth solving, how it was modelled, at what scale, and what the model actually decided.

Where results exist, they are stated. Where a project is still running, that is stated too rather than filled with an approximate figure.

The research section carries six projects in full. Four further pieces of work are listed in brief. Source notebooks and reports are in the public repository.
