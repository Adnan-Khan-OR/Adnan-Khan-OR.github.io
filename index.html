Stochastic Power Generation Capacity Planning

01–03/2026
Two-Stage SPBendersCVaRCVXPY

Two-stage stochastic linear program with recourse selecting installed capacity across four generator technologies before random regional demand realises, with load shedding guaranteeing complete recourse.

Solved as a sample average approximation in extensive form, then by a Benders L-shaped decomposition with optimality cuts derived from capacity constraint duals, and extended to a risk-averse CVaR objective.

Maintenance Planning for Offshore Wind Turbines

11–12/2025
MILPSchedulingPyomo · Gurobi

Mixed-integer linear program scheduling preventive and corrective maintenance under crew availability, vessel travel and maintenance window constraints.

Turbine degradation modelled through discrete health states, minimising total lifecycle cost across maintenance, transport and outage penalties, validated with scenario-based sensitivity analysis on failure frequency and preventive thresholds.

Stochastic Machine Replacement Policy

02/2026
MDPValue IterationPython

Infinite-horizon Markov decision process over ten degradation states determining when to replace a production machine to maximise expected discounted profit.

Bellman optimality equation solved by value iteration, with sensitivity analysis across replacement cost, discount factor and state transition probabilities. Runs live in the Lab above.

Accelerated Life Testing & Warranty Optimization

12/2025–01/2026
WeibullALTReliability

Right-censored Weibull modelling of LED degradation under multiple temperature and current stress conditions, with Arrhenius and multi-stress ALT models predicting lifetime at normal operating conditions.

Estimated MTTF, median life and B10, then built a reliability-driven warranty and pricing optimization model identifying a premium long-warranty strategy at low warranty risk.

Master's research

Thesis

Integrated Vehicle Routing, Order Assignment and Optimization for Resilient Omnichannel Distribution Networks

CentraleSupélecSupervisor · Prof. Jakob Puchinger2026 MILP · Vehicle Routing · Pyomo · Gurobi
  • Mixed-integer linear program integrating vehicle routing and order assignment decisions across an omnichannel distribution network, extending the capacitated vehicle routing problem with channel-specific allocation constraints.
  • Benchmarked the integrated formulation against a sequential baseline in which routing and assignment are optimised separately, isolating the value of joint decision-making.
  • Quantified network resilience by re-solving under disruption scenarios, measuring the trade-off between total distribution cost and maintained service levels.
