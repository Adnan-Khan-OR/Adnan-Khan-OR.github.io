On this site

This site documents the optimisation work behind my curriculum vitae. Each project is written the way I would explain it to a colleague: what the problem was, why it was worth solving, how it was modelled, at what scale, and what the model actually decided.

Where results exist, they are stated. Where a project is still running, that is stated too rather than filled with an approximate figure.

The research section carries six projects in full. Four further pieces of work are listed in brief. Source notebooks and reports are in the public repository.
